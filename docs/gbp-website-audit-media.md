Google Business Profile does not accept automated video posts without OAuth.
Use these URLs and paste the post text in [Google Business Profile Manager](https://business.google.com/).

## Manual GBP steps

1. Open [Google Business Profile Manager](https://business.google.com/) and select **DigiSol**.
2. Go to **Posts** (or **Videos** / media, depending on the UI).
3. Create a new post and **upload the same MP4** (`digisol-website-audit.mp4` from your Desktop or from the site URL above).
4. Paste the post text below. Include the page URL so Google can associate the video with DigiSol’s site.
5. Publish. Optionally pin or boost if available in your market.
6. After a day or two, confirm the post appears on the public GBP listing and that Search Console / GBP insights show engagement.

## Why the site page matters

- `/media/website-audit` has VideoObject JSON-LD, canonical meta, and sitemap entry so Google can discover the video beyond GBP.
- Homepage `#media` embeds the same file after Services for on-site viewers.
- Organization schema on the site points at this media page via `subjectOf`.
